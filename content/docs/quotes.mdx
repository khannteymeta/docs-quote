# Wisdom in Quotes

A compilation of sentences that changed the way I think, work, and live.

---

## On Growth and Habits

> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."
> — **James Clear**, *Atomic Habits*

### Reflection
Most people spend time writing goals instead of designing the routine that naturally leads to them. I started writing 100 words a day instead of aiming to "write a book." It takes the pressure off and builds consistency.

---

## On Focus and Time

> "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it."
> — **Seneca**, *On the Shortness of Life*

### Reflection
Time is our only truly non-renewable resource. We protect our money and property fiercely, but let people steal our attention and time without a second thought. Setting boundaries on notification settings was my step to live this quote.

---

## On Overcoming Challenges

> "The obstacle in the path becomes the path. Never forget, within every obstacle is an opportunity to improve our condition."
> — **Marcus Aurelius**, *Meditations*

### Reflection
Whenever something goes wrong, I try to ask myself: "What does this problem make possible?" It shifts the mindset from feeling like a victim to finding an active solution.
